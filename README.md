# 🦒🔥 3D LLM Comedy Benchmark Arena

An interactive split-screen **Three.js (WebGL) Viral Benchmark Studio** for creating side-by-side 3D AI model comedy comparisons for **X (Twitter)** and **LinkedIn** (e.g., `Opus 5.5 vs Gemini 4 Carbon`, `Claude 4.6 Opus vs Gemini 4 Argon`, `GPT-5.5 Turbo vs Gemma 4 31B-IT`).

🔗 **Live Studio (GitHub Pages):** [https://robinamirbahar.github.io/llm-3d-comedy-arena/](https://robinamirbahar.github.io/llm-3d-comedy-arena/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **8 Funny 3D Procedural Animal Benchmarks**:
  1. 🦒 **Galloping Giraffe** (*Spaghetti Knees vs. Majestic National Geographic Gallop*)
  2. 🦖 **T-Rex Trying to Sprint** (*Tiny Arms Panic Attack vs. Jurassic Park Apex Predator*)
  3. 🐈 **Orange Cat 3 AM Zoomies** (*Vibrating 4-Polygon Loaf vs. Aerodynamic Parkour Champion*)
  4. 🦆 **Duck Late for Daily Standup** (*360° Head Spin vs. CEO Speed Waddle*)
  5. 🦀 **Crab Rave Party** (*Boiled Triangle Crisis vs. 120 BPM Mainstage Headliner*)
  6. 🐧 **Penguin Belly Toboggan** (*Tumbling Bowling Pin vs. Olympic Gold Medal Glider*)
  7. 🐉 **Cyber Mech Dragon** (*Flying Toaster Coughing Cubes vs. 18-Joint Procedural Shenron*)
  8. 🐙 **Octopus Coding at 400 WPM** (*Tangled Wet Spaghetti vs. 8 Inverse-Kinematic Tentacles*)
- **12+ AI Model Presets & 1-Click Matchup Pills**:
  - Instant presets for `Opus 5.5`, `Gemini 4 Carbon`, `Gemini 4 Argon`, `Gemma 4 31B-IT`, `Gemma 4 Multi-LoRA`, `Claude 4.6 Opus`, `GPT-5.5 Turbo`, `DeepSeek R2`, `Llama 4 405B`, and `Grok 4 Unhinged`.
- **Comedy Controls**:
  - **👀 Googly Derp Eyes Toggle**: Adds spinning cross-eyed googly eyes to the left model.
  - **💬 Roast Captions Toggle**: Displays dynamic comedy roast badges above each model.
  - **⚡ Chaos / Derp Multiplier**: Scale physics wobble from `1x Normal` up to `4.2x MAX BRAINROT 💀`.
  - **🔄 Synchronized 3D Orbit & Scroll Zoom**: Drag anywhere to rotate both cameras in sync, scroll wheel to zoom in/out, or click **Fit / Reset View**.
- **⏺ 1-Click 60 FPS `.webm` Video Export**:
  - Records an 8-second side-by-side 1280×800 video with headers, roast badges, divider line, and your custom watermark (`@RobinaMirbahar`) ready to upload directly to X or LinkedIn.
